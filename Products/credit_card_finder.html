01The yearly figure

For each card, we take what you spend in each area (online shopping, food, groceries, fuel, travel and bills) and apply the card's rate for that area. Where the card has no special rate, its general rate applies. Rewards stop at the card's monthly limit. Then the annual fee and the markup on foreign-currency spend are taken off.

Back to you = spend in each area × the card's rate for it (up to the monthly limit) − annual fee − forex markup

02Fees

If a paid card drops its fee once you spend a set amount in a year, and your spend gets there, the fee counts as zero. The one-time joining fee is shown separately. Banks usually add 18% GST on fees, which isn't included.

03Who can get it

Cards are left out when your income is below the bank's minimum or your age is outside its limits. Where the bank hasn't published a limit, the card stays in.

04If you don't pay in full

Cards are sorted by interest rate instead, lowest first. Most cards here charge 3.5% to 4% interest a month, far more than any reward gives back.

05Not counted

Extra rewards at specific shops or apps, welcome gifts, lounge visits, fuel surcharge waivers and milestone bonuses. A card with these can be worth more than its figure here. Points and miles are counted at the cash value the data gives them.

06Where the data comes from

All card facts come from the CardAdvisor India Credit Card Facts Dataset, used under CC BY 4.0. A copy is refreshed from their file every day. Infinity by Yam doesn't check each figure against the banks and earns nothing from any of them.

This is information, not advice. Banks change fees and rewards often. Always read the bank's own terms before you apply.
