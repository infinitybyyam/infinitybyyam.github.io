Learning

An interactive series that teaches finance and valuation concepts by letting you change the numbers yourself and watch the result respond.

In Progress

Built from first principles

Finance doesn’t have to live in heavy textbooks or dead formulas. This is a hands-on way to learn finance concepts by playing with the numbers yourself. Instead of memorizing equations, drag sliders, watch how the outputs shift, and actually get a feel for how the math works in practice.

  1. 01
    Built From Scratch: No shortcuts or hand-waving. Every concept is broken down from the ground up so you see where the numbers actually come from.
  2. 02
    Move the Sliders: Tweak variables in real time and watch the models recalculate instantly right in front of you.
  3. 03
    See the Impact: Clear, real-time visuals designed to help finance concepts actually stick.

The full picture, not one number

Financial products are often sold on one shiny headline number. These calculators dig deeper, working through each layer behind that number with your own inputs, so you know what you’re actually walking away with.

  1. 01
    Your Numbers, Your Reality: No hidden assumptions. You can tweak every variable to match your situation.
  2. 02
    Real-World Value: The factors that usually get left out are built in, so the final number is closer to what you actually keep.
  3. 03
    Ranges, Not Guarantees: See how much a plan shifts when things don’t go according to a single fixed estimate.
